# DUNEFALL

Original 3D desert mech & transforming combat car battlefield built with Three.js + Vite + TypeScript for [start.ng](https://start.ng) and Vercel.

## Game Modes

### 1. Story Campaign — *The Glass Reach* (5 Chapters + Boss Finale)
Push back Warden Vex's Crimson Directorate across Sector 09 with diegetic radio comms (**Commander Kora**, **Vanguard-OS**, and **Warden Vex**), custom atmosphere per sector, 3D objective waypoints, and permanent between-chapter tactical refit choices:
- **OP-01 · First Contact** *(Outpost Ridge)* — Break the Crimson recon vanguard, then morph to Car (`T`) to salvage 3 emerald Relay Cores.
- **OP-02 · Signal Fire** *(Red Canyon)* — Capture and hold 2 Helios Relay Towers inside pulsing perimeter rings against Crimson Breachers.
- **OP-03 · Iron Convoy** *(Shattered Basin)* — Escort heavy 6-wheel core hauler **ATLAS-6** through a desert ambush to the northern extraction gate.
- **OP-04 · Scorched Earth** *(Ash Caldera)* — Dodge telegraphed crimson artillery impact rings and shatter 3 Crimson Artillery Pylons.
- **OP-05 · Sunbreaker** *(The Glass Reach)* — Two-phase boss showdown against **Warden Vex** piloting the double-scale dreadnought **SOLARIS-0** (with Phase II *Solar Overdrive*).

### 2. Endless Skirmish
Classic escalating wave survival with emerald Energy Core upgrades (Tier 1–5), Support Squads, Missile Pods, and `start.ng` global leaderboard tracking.

## Controls
- `WASD` / Arrows — Move mech or drive 6-wheel combat car
- `Mouse` + `LMB` — Aim & fire active weapon
- `RMB` — Hold frontal energy Brace Shield
- `T` — Transform between War Mech and 6-wheel Combat Car
- `G / H / J / K / L` — Switch weapons (`VANGUARD`, `BASTION`, `SUNLANCE`, `RAILBREAKER`, `CRATERFALL`)
- `F` — Homing missile salvo · `Shift + A / D` — Side-roll dodge · `X / Z` — Siege / Strider stance
- `Q` — Cycle target lock · `4` — Support Squad · `5` — Missile Pod · `Esc` — Settings / Switch Mode
